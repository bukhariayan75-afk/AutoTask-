FAQ

Quick answers about AutoTask.

What is AutoTask?

AutoTask is a lightweight mouse recorder and auto clicker designed to make repetitive tasks easier.

Which Windows versions are supported?

AutoTask is made for Windows 10 and Windows 11.

How does recording work?

Press Record (F8), perform your mouse actions, then press Play (F7) to replay them. The Recorder shows how many events you have recorded.

How do I save my recordings?

Use Save .rec to store a recording as a .rec file, and Load .rec to open it again later.

Which keyboard shortcuts are available?

F8 controls recording and F7 controls playback.
